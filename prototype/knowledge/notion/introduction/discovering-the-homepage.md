## Top summary metrics — Discovering the Homepage

These are the top summary **metrics** we highlight:

[image: summary metric tiles]

- **Live Events** – Events currently active across the platform.
- **Total Events** – All events initiated during the selected date range.
- **Event Closed** – Events that have been concluded.
- **Bids Received** – Total bids submitted by vendors.
- **Purchase Value** – Aggregate value of awarded purchases.
- **New Vendors** – Vendors newly onboarded during this period.
- **Engaged Vendors** – Vendors who actively placed bids.

## Top Bar — Discovering the Homepage

[image: top bar icons]

**Icons** *(starting from the Left)*: Pending Approvals, Notifications, Give Feedback / View Support Tickets, Chat, User Profile

#### Pending Approvals

On clicking **Pending Approvals**, a side panel is opened.

[image: Pending Approvals side panel]

Here you can take quick Action (Reject / Approve) on any approvals pending on you. You can see some overview summarised details, but you can always click on **View More Details** for more information. This is a helpful Task list that you can easily clear away.

To learn more about Approvals, you can visit the [insert page link for Approvals Section]

#### Notifications

On clicking the notifications icon, a notification drawer opens up.

[image: notification drawer]

Here you can see different notifications that have been triggered by the system and things that might need your attention. You can also click on Mark As Read, which is highlighted, to mark them as read and remove them from your notification drawer. This is similar to notifications that are being sent via email.

#### Feedback Icon

On clicking this icon a short message pops up on screen.

[image: feedback pop-up]

Here you're prompted to give your Feedback regarding the platform or even view active Tickets that have been raised.

On clicking 'Give feedback', a pop-up appears where you can either select whether your feedback is regarding an issue, an idea, or anything else. After you click either 'issue', 'idea', or 'other', you can then type in the text box in detail what your feedback is and click on 'Send Feedback' to submit your response.

If it is regarding an Issue, you will be raising a ticket which you can later track.

[image: Give feedback pop-up with issue / idea / other]
[image: feedback text box and Send Feedback button]

#### Chats

On clicking this icon, you are taken to Procol's Internal communication chat module, where you can start chats with users of the platform and even vendors.

[image: chat module]

#### User Profile Icon

When you hover over the User Profile Icon, a sub-menu is opened with your Name and Organisation Details on top. Under this there are 3 options.

[image: user profile sub-menu]

- **My profile**: On clicking this, a side panel is opened with all your user details. Here you can edit your Name and Profile picture. But in most cases are restricted from editing your email and phone number. You can press on "Edit" to unfreeze these fields and then press on save when you're done editing.
  [image: My profile side panel]
- **Keyboard Shortcuts**: When you click on this option, a list of Keyboard shortcuts that you can use for specific actions opens up. This will make repetitive actions easier for users. These Shortcuts can be used during Sourcing. To learn more about Sourcing click here [insert link]
  [image: keyboard shortcuts list]
- **Sign Out**: This option signs you out of the Procol platform and takes you to the sign-in screen.
  [image: sign-in screen after sign out]
