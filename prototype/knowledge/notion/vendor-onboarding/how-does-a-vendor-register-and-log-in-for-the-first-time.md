# How does a Vendor register and log in for the first time?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How does a Vendor register and log in for the first time? (https://app.notion.com/p/33492226c8b282cabede813d37d7678e) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Vendor Onboarding

Vendors access Procol's supplier portal via www.trade.procol.in. This portal is where they can view Onboard themselves, event invitations, submit bids, etc.

## Who Is This For? — How does a Vendor register and log in for the first time?

Vendors who have been invited to join Procol and need to sign in to the Procol platform for the first time.

This guide explains how a vendor can **log in to the system for the first time**, either through an **email invitation** or by directly accessing the site.

[image: vendor portal sign-in page]

## Login Methods — How does a Vendor register and log in for the first time?

1. **Via Email Invitation**
   - When invited by a buyer, vendors receive an **email with a login link**.
   - Clicking this link takes them directly to the login or setup page, where they can enter their details and set a password.
2. **Via Direct Access to www.trade.procol.in**
   - Vendors can also go directly to the supplier portal anytime.
   - They'll be prompted to log in using:
     - **Email address** **(must be used for the first time)**, or
     - **Phone number** (for consecutive logins if the phone number is added).

> **Note.** Only users who have been invited by a buyer will be able to access the platform.

## First-time sign-in steps — How does a Vendor register and log in for the first time?

1. Click on Continue with email (if logging in for the first time, log in with the same email where you have received the invitation)
   [image: Continue with email]
   > This is what is shown on the screen next
   [image: email entry screen]
2. Click on the blue "Sent OTP" button
   [image: Sent OTP button]
3. Enter the OTP received in your email.
   > You can retry "send OTP" if the OTP is not received or has expired.
   [image: OTP entry]
   > You are taken to this **Vendor Sign up** page, where some details are already pre-filled by the buyer who has invited you.
   > You can edit these details except for the email id.
   > It's optional to add a contact phone number at this stage.
   [image: Vendor Sign up page]
4. Click on the checkbox next to the "I agree to the terms of use,…."
   [image: terms checkbox]
5. Click on the blue "Submit" Button
   [image: Submit button]
   > You are then logged into the platform and this is the screen that is visible
   > If the buyer has not initiated your onboarding, then you may see no live events. There is no further action to be taken when Onboarding is not enabled.
   [image: vendor dashboard after first login]
