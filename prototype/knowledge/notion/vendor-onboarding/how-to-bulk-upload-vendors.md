# How to Bulk Upload Vendors?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Bulk Upload Vendors? (https://app.notion.com/p/c5092226c8b283158c3301791b339247) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Vendor Onboarding

Procol allows you to onboard multiple vendors at once by using the Bulk Upload feature. Instead of adding vendors one by one, you can fill in an Excel sheet with vendor details and upload it, saving time and effort.

## Step-by-Step Process — How to Bulk Upload Vendors?

1. Go to the **Vendors tab** using the left navigation panel.
   [image: Vendors tab in the left navigation]
   [image: Vendors page]
2. On the **Vendors page**, click on the **Bulk Upload** button located at the top-right, next to Save as Excel.
   [image: Bulk Upload button next to Save as Excel]
3. You will be taken to the **Bulk Upload page**, where you can view a history of past bulk uploads.
   [image: Bulk Upload page with upload history]
4. Click on **New Upload** at the top-right.
   [image: New Upload button]
   [image: New Upload modal]
5. Download the **Sample Template** and fill in the vendor details in the Excel sheet as per the format.
   [image: Sample Template download]
   [image: sample template sheet]
   [image: filled sheet]
6. Upload the completed Excel sheet back into the module.
   [image: uploaded file in the modal]
7. Press **OK** to trigger the bulk upload process.
   [image: OK button]

> **Note.** Once the upload is processed successfully, all the vendors will appear in your **Prospects tab**.
> [image: vendors in the Prospects tab]
