# Pre-Qualification (PQ) Score

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — Pre-Qualification (PQ) Score (https://app.notion.com/p/de692226c8b283afb03c8136ece9e277) · last edited 2026-04-20
Section: Vendor Onboarding

The Pre-Qualification (PQ) Score is a vendor evaluation system that assigns a numerical rating to vendors based on their submissions. This helps buyers assess vendor quality, perform risk assessments, and compare vendors objectively before onboarding or awarding them business.

## How to Use PQ Score in Procol — Pre-Qualification (PQ) Score

1. **Navigate to Vendor Page**
   - Go to the **Vendors** tab from the left navigation panel.
   - Open a vendor profile and click on the **Onboarding Details** tab.
     [image: Onboarding Details tab]
2. **Start Evaluation**
   - On the top-right, click **Start Evaluation**.
   - Scroll down to find the **Evaluation Matrix Type** dropdown.
     [image: Start Evaluation and Evaluation Matrix Type]
3. **Select Evaluation Matrix**
   [image: Evaluation Matrix Type dropdown]
   - Choose the relevant evaluation matrix from the dropdown.
     [image: matrix options]
   - Once selected, multiple **sections and questions** will appear.
     [image: sections and questions]
4. **Fill Responses and Scores**
   - For each question, enter your response and assign a score.
     [image: response and score entry]
   - Example: *Active years in business*
     - **7+ years** → Max score 5
     - **5–7 years** → Max score 3
     - **3–5 years** → Max score 2
     [image: example question scoring]
   - Question types can vary:
     - Yes/No
     - Multiple Choice (MCQs)
     - Binary Questions
     [image: question types]
   - Scores per section are auto-summed at the top of each section.
     [image: section score total]
5. **Approval Process**
   - Once scoring is complete, click **Approve** at the bottom.
   - A side panel opens for **Send for Approval**.
   - Add **Category** and **Location** if needed and click on Next
   - Review the approval workflow, add remarks, and select an approver (e.g., *Adnan Tahir*).
   - Click on **Continue** to submit for approval.
   [image: Send for Approval side panel steps]
6. **View Vendor Score**
   - After approval, the system generates a **Total Vendor Score**.
   - Example: *95 (Green - High Quality)*.
   [image: Total Vendor Score]

## PQ Score Color Coding — Pre-Qualification (PQ) Score

- **Green (Above 60):** Vendor is a good choice.
- **Yellow (40–60):** Vendor is average; caution advised.
- **Red (Below 40):** Vendor is high-risk.

## Benefits of PQ Score — Pre-Qualification (PQ) Score

- Standardized vendor evaluation.
- Helps identify high-risk vendors early.
- Assists in fair, comparative decision-making.
- Can be used as a **decisive factor** before vendor onboarding.
