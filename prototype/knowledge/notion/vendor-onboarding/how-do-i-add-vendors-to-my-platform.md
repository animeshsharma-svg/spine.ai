# How do I add Vendors to my Platform?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I add Vendors to my Platform? (https://app.notion.com/p/b4f92226c8b283d3a4e701a2eee1071e) · last edited 2026-04-23
Section: Vendor Onboarding

Inviting vendors to the platform gives them access to Procol's **Vendor Dashboard** at www.trade.procol.in. Once invited (and optionally onboarded), vendors can start participating in sourcing events they are invited to — including RFQs, RFPs, and Auctions.

> **Note.** Already Invited? To understand how to onboard your Vendors, go to [link to another Knowledge Base article]

## Who Is This For? — How do I add Vendors to my Platform?

Buyers, Procurement Managers, Vendor Onboarding Team

## Steps to Invite Vendors — How do I add Vendors to my Platform?

1. Navigate to the Vendors page from the left navigation panel.
   [image: Vendors in the left navigation]
   > This how the Vendor Screen looks like (Onboarding not enabled)
   [image: Vendors screen with onboarding not enabled]
2. Click on the "+ **Invite Vendor**" button.
   [image: + Invite Vendor button]
   > A Side Drawer Opens up, that asks for basic details of the Vendor you are inviting as well as his contact details.
   [image: Invite Vendor side drawer]
3. In \* POC - Full Name, enter the full name of the "Person of Contact" at the Vendor's business.
   [image: POC - Full Name field]
4. In Email, enter the Email to which all communications must be sent, and the email he will use to log in to the platform.
   [image: Email field]
5. In the "Choose vendor profile" here you can choose the type of Vendor.
   - **Manufacture/Trader** - If he is the direct seller of the goods.
   - **Broker** - A middleman organisation or individual that connects you to multiple sellers and bids on your behalf.
   [image: Choose vendor profile]
6. This is a non-mandatory field to add a Vendor Code (as per your ERP Vendor Master / Internal Identification code). This code can be updated at a later time, or automatically via **Onboarding with Integrations.**
   [image: Vendor Code field]
7. You can search and select Categories that this Vendor deals in to map him to those categories. This is non mandatory at this stage and can be done at a later time
   [image: Categories selector]
8. In the "Invite Vendor via" field, your selected choice changes the following fields.
   - If you select **Tax ID**, you will have to select the Business Location (Country), which will then show the appropriate Tax ID field that needs to be entered (VAT, GSTIN, etc)
     [image: Tax ID with Business Location]
     [image: Tax ID field for the selected country]
   - If you select Enter details manually, you will be required to enter 3 fields. Companu Name, Address, and City (from a dropdown)
     [image: manual details — Company Name, Address, City]

After filling in all the above details, you can then click on the blue "**Next**" button,

[image: Next button]

You will see a summary like so before you invite this Vendor

[image: invite summary]

After reviewing this you can confirm and click on the blue "Invite" button

[image: Invite button]

A Success message will displayed at the top of the screen, confirming that the invite has been sent.

[image: success message]

The Vendor will recieve a link to the vendor dashboard site where he can now use the same email to log into his account for the first time, and start using the platform.

> **Note.** If there is no Vendor Onboarding, the Vendor is directly Joined. If there is Onboarding, then the Vendor is now a prospect, and will only be a joined vendor post-onboarding.

> **Note.** To see how Onboarding is done, check out [link to another Knowledge Base article]
