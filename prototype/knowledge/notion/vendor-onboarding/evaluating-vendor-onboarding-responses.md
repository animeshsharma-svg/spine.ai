## Who Is This For? — Evaluating Vendor Onboarding Responses

Buyers, Procurement Managers, Vendor Onboarding Team

> This guide starts at the Vendor's profile page. In the Onboarding Details Page. To reach till here, you can refer to [link to another Knowledge Base article]

[image: Onboarding Details page on the vendor profile]

## Steps to Evaluate — Evaluating Vendor Onboarding Responses

1. Here you have the option to force start the Evaluation time by clicking on **"Start Evaluation"**
   Alternatively you can let the submission time expire and evaluation time will auto-start.
   [image: Start Evaluation button]
2. While scrolling through the responses, you can easily jump to a different section by clicking on "Jump Section" and selecting the section you want to jump to.
   [image: Jump Section]
3. After reviewing the response you can either **Approve** or **Reject** the Vendor.
   [image: Approve / Reject]
4. On Approval, the send for Approval Tab will open.
   If Buyer Added Attributes (An Integration based feature is enabled, there will be a form for the buyer to fill here)
   [image: Buyer Added Attributes form]
   [image: Buyer Added Attributes form, continued]
   You can then click on "**Next**" to continue
   [image: Next button]
5. You now see a summary of the Vendor/Prospect that you want to have approved.
   You can add a Category and Location for this vendor here.
   After you're done press on "**Next**".
   [image: prospect summary with Category and Location]
6. Finally, you can add remarks and observe the approval workflow to be triggered.
   Click on **Continue**
   [image: remarks and approval workflow]

> **Note.** The prospect has been sent for Approval
> [image: sent for approval confirmation]

7. The respective approver can then **"Approve / Reject"** from their account.
   [image: approver Approve / Reject]
   [image: approver view]

Once approved, the Vendor is now onboarded and placed into **"Joined"** Status.

[image: vendor in Joined status]

> **Note.** He is now eligible to be invited to participate in any event
