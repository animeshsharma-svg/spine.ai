## Who Is This For? — Onboarding as a Vendor

Vendors who have been invited to join Procol and need to complete onboarding before participating in sourcing events.

## What Is Vendor Onboarding? — Onboarding as a Vendor

Vendor onboarding is a step required by the buyer to gather important details about a supplier before allowing participation in sourcing events or awarding purchase orders.

Once a vendor logs into the Procol Vendor Portal (www.trade.procol.in), they may be prompted to complete an onboarding form before becoming eligible to transact.

This form typically includes:

- Company profile and contact information
- GST and PAN details
- Banking information
- Compliance documents or certificates

Until onboarding is completed and approved by the buyer, the vendor will not be able to access events or receive POs.

## Steps to Complete Vendor Onboarding — Onboarding as a Vendor

> When a Vendor Onboarding Event has started, this is what the Vendor sees.

[image: vendor dashboard with Vendor Onboarding Event card]

1. Click on the Vendor Onboarding Event Card
   [image: Vendor Onboarding Event card]
   > This is the Event Overview screen that opens after clicking on the Event card
   [image: Event Overview screen]
   > This is an important choice to make that inform's the buyer your willingness to participate in this event.
   [image: participation choice]
2. If you wish to continue, click on the blue **"I will participate"** button
   [image: I will participate button]
3. If not automatically taken to the next tab, click on the tab next to "Event Overview"
   [image: tab next to Event Overview]
   > This is the how screen would look. This is the onboarding form that you need to fill and submit. Scroll through the various sections and be sure to fill all the Mandatory fields.
4. Fill the form
   [image: onboarding form]
5. After filling in the form, scroll to the bottom and click on "Submit"
   [image: Submit button]
   > You will get a confirmation at the top of the screen.
   [image: confirmation message]

> **Note.** You have successfully submitted your vendor onboarding form response! Now we wait for evaluation to be done by the buyer.
> [image: submitted state]

> **Note.** If the submission time is still running, you can still make changes and re-submit your response.
> [image: re-submit option]
