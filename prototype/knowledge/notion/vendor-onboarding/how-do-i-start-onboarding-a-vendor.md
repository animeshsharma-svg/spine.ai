# How do I Start Onboarding a Vendor?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I Start Onboarding a Vendor? (https://app.notion.com/p/19d92226c8b2832b9839013a628d17ee) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Vendor Onboarding

Onboarding vendors happens after they are invited to the platform and given access to Procol's **Vendor Dashboard** at www.trade.procol.in. If Onboarding is enabled, until onboarded, vendors cannot participate in sourcing events they are invited to — including RFQs, RFPs, and Auctions.

> **Note.** Not Invited? To understand how to invite your Vendors, go to [link to another Knowledge Base article]

## Who Is This For? — How do I Start Onboarding a Vendor?

Buyers, Procurement Managers, Vendor Onboarding Team

## Steps to Onboard Vendors — How do I Start Onboarding a Vendor?

1. Navigate to the Vendors page from the left navigation panel.
   [image: Vendors in the left navigation]
   > This how the Vendor Screen looks like when Onboarding is enabled.
   [image: Vendors screen with onboarding enabled]
2. Navigate to the Prospects Tab.
   > **Note.** Prospects are those Vendors who have been invited but not Onboarded yet
   [image: Prospects tab]
3. Select the vendors you would like to Start Onboarding. You can select multiple at once.
   [image: selecting prospects]
4. Click on the blue "Start Onboarding" button.
   The number shows how many vendors you have selected for Onboarding
   [image: Start Onboarding button with count]
   > **The "Onboarding Prospects" Side panel shows up**
   [image: Onboarding Prospects side panel]
5. Select a Template under "Onboarding Template".
   [image: Onboarding Template selector]
6. You can also schedule this event by changing its Start time, Duration (End time), and Evaluation time.
   [image: Start time, Duration, Evaluation time]
7. You can review which Vendors are being sent to the Onboarding Event in the Prospects
8. You can find the Prospect now in the **"Onboarding in Progress"** Tab
   [image: Onboarding in Progress tab]
9. Click on the "Test User" (Company Name of the Prospect being Onboarded)
   > You will be taken to the Vendor Profile Page
   [image: Vendor Profile Page]
10. Click on the "Onboarding Details" Tab
    [image: Onboarding Details tab]
    > Here you can see the Vendor Responses , once they are submitted by them.
11. If needed, you can also extend the submission time to allow the vendor to have more time to submit their response.
    [image: extend submission time]

> **Note.** You have successfully started the onboarding for a vendor!
