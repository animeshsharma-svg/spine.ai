## Budget creation form — P2P section overview

Here you have the option to select the department name. You can select the type and the fiscal year range. The status will automatically be in the draft state for now. You can ignore fields like allocated budget, spent budget, and balance amount as these are aggregate fields that will be calculated later. You can also select the participant teams, and this form can be customizable for different clients. Here you select the participant team and you can also select all the participant teams depending on how many teams you want to allocate to the budget. And you can also click on the 'Show more' button which shows you the rest of the fields that may have been previously hidden.

Underneath, you can see three tabs:

1. Budget category list
2. Budget update request list
3. Purchase acquisition

In the budget category list, you can then click on the 'Plus new' button and here you can select a category type in the budget allocated.
