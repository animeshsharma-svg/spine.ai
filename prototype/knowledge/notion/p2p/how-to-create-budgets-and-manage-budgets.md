# How to Create Budgets and Manage Budgets

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Create Budgets and Manage Budgets (https://app.notion.com/p/b5892226c8b283db8d3b014ac12c4823) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: P2P › Budget Management

In this knowledge base page, you will learn how to create budgets, manage them, and update them. Budgets help set limits on procurement or spending for a specific department, location, or branch. They can be defined based on various variables such as department, location, or branch. You can also track allocated budgets, spending, and balances, as well as manage updates and approvals.

## Step-by-Step Process — Create and Manage Budgets

1. Navigate to the **Budget Management** page using the **left-side navigation panel**.
   [image: Budget Management in the left-side navigation]
2. On the Budget Management page, you will find two tabs:
   [image: the Budget Management page tabs]
   - **Budget Department List** – Shows current budgets defined department-wise, their status, period type, fiscal year range, allocated budget, spent budget, and participant teams.
   - **Budget Update Request List** – Shows all submitted requests for updating budgets.
3. To create a new budget, click the **+ New** button.
   [image: the + New button]
4. On the **Budget Creation** page, fill in the required details:
   [image: the Budget Creation page]
   - Department Name
   - Period Type
   - Fiscal Year Range
5. Click **Show More** to reveal additional customizable fields.
   [image: the Show More control]
6. These fields will be automatically calculated based on future entries we will make, for now ignore them:
   - Allocated Budget
   - Spent Budget
   - Balance Amount
   [image: the auto-calculated amount fields]
7. Assign participant teams (up to 4) by selecting from the dropdown lists.
   [image: participant team dropdowns]
8. Note that **Approval Status** and **Budget Status** are determined automatically based on system rules.
   [image: Approval Status and Budget Status fields]
9. At the bottom of the page, you will see three tabs:
   [image: the three tabs at the bottom of the Budget Creation page]
   - **Budget Category List** – Create the actual Budgets specific to Categories
   - **Budget Update Request List** – View and manage budget update requests.
   - **Purchase Requisition** – Track PRs linked to the budget.
10. To add a new budget category, click the **+ New** button under the **Budget Category List** tab.
11. Fill in the Category Name, Budget Allocated, and Budget Spent. (The Balance Amount will be auto calculated (Allocated - Spent))
    [image: a budget category row]
    [image: filled budget category row with balance]
12. To delete a budget category, click the **Delete** icon next to it.
    [image: the Delete icon on a budget category]
13. To add another Budget you can click the **+ New** button and repeat the above process again. You can add multiple bugdets at once.
    [image: multiple budget category rows]
14. Once you're done you can press on the **Submit Budget** button.
    [image: the Submit Budget button]
15. A preview of the **Approval Workflow** will appear for you to review.
    [image: the Approval Workflow preview]
16. Then Click **Submit**.
    [image: the Submit button]

> **Note.** An **Approval Note** is generated showing the budget details and approval workflow. Once approved the Budget is successfully created!
> [image: the generated Approval Note]
