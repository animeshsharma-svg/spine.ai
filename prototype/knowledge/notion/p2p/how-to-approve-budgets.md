# How to Approve Budgets

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Approve Budgets (https://app.notion.com/p/f4492226c8b2835a8247014776b1742d) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: P2P › Budget Management

This guide explains how approvers can review and approve budget creation requests in Procol. Once a budget is submitted, it follows the configured approval workflow. Approvers can view budget details, add remarks, and approve or reject the request.

🫂 User Persona - Approvers

## Steps — Approve Budgets

1. Log in to your Procol account.
2. From the left-side navigation panel, go to the **Approvals** tab.
   [image: the Approvals tab in the left-side navigation]
3. In the **Approvals** tab, locate the budget approval request pending your action.
   [image: pending budget approval requests]
4. Click on the relevant budget approval request to open it.
   [image: opening a budget approval request]
5. The **Approval Note** page will appear, showing:
   - **Overview** section with budget details.
   - **Details** section listing the budget categories, amounts, and workflow information.
   [image: the Approval Note page]
6. Review the budget information carefully to ensure it meets requirements.
7. (Optional) Add your remarks in the remarks field.
   [image: the remarks field]
8. Click **Approve** to approve the request or choose **Reject** if the budget needs changes.
   [image: Approve and Reject buttons]
9. Once your approval is submitted, the workflow automatically moves to the next approver in the sequence (if any).
   [image: workflow advancing to the next approver]
10. When all approvers in the workflow have approved, the budget status updates to **Approved**, and the budget becomes active for the specified department and categories.
    [image: budget showing Approved status]
    [image: approved budget in the list]
