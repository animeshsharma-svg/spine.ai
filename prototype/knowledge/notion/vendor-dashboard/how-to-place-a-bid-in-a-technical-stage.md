# How to place a bid in a Technical Stage

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to place a bid in a Technical Stage (https://app.notion.com/p/50f92226c8b282f3bc690133226cadc6) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Vendor Dashboard

A Technical Stage event allows you to submit product specifications, documents, and other technical details **before** the commercial round begins. This ensures that your offerings meet the buyer's requirements from a technical standpoint.

## User Personas — Place a bid in a Technical Stage

Vendors, Brokers

## Steps — Place a bid in a Technical Stage

> **Note.** This is the Event Overview screen that opens after clicking on the Event card.

[image: Event Overview screen of a Technical Stage event]

> **Note.** This is an important choice to make that informs the buyer your willingness to participate in this event.

[image: participation choice on the Event Overview screen]

1. If you wish to place a bid in this event, click on the blue **"I will participate"** button.
   [image: "I will participate" button]
2. If not automatically taken to the "Technical Stage", click on the tab next to "Details".
   [image: Technical Stage tab next to Details]

> **Note.** This is the Technical Stage screen, here you fill Vendor Assessments and Line Item Specifications.

[image: Technical Stage screen with Vendor Assessments and Line Item Specifications]

3. Click on "Select an option" to fill out your vendor responses. Click on one of the following options like manufacturer, trader, seller, etc.
   [image: "Select an option" dropdown with vendor-type options]
4. You also have a **"⚡Quick Fill"** option that you can use to immediately copy-paste values specified by the buyer into the vendor's responses.
   - A. From the options, you can select **"Same as requested"** to immediately copy-paste the values for all the specified questions.
     [image: Quick Fill menu with "Same as requested"]
   - B. Alternatively, you can also regret items by pressing on **'Regret Item'**, which means that you will not be placing a bid for this item.
     [image: Quick Fill menu with "Regret Item"]
5. After filling in the form, scroll to the bottom and click on "Submit".
   [image: Submit button at the bottom of the form]

> **Note.** You will get a confirmation at the top of the screen.

[image: confirmation shown at the top of the screen]

> **Note.** You have successfully submitted your Technical stage response! Now we wait for evaluation to be done by the buyer/technical Evaluator.

> **Note.** If the submission time is still running, you can still make changes and re-submit your response.

[image: re-submit option while submission time is running]
