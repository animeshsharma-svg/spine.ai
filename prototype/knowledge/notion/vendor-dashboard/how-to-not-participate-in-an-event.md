## Steps — How to not Participate in an Event

> **Note.** This is the Event Overview screen that opens after clicking on the Event card.

[image: Event Overview screen]

> **Note.** This is an important choice to make that informs the buyer your willingness to participate in this event.

[image: participation choice on the Event Overview screen]

1. If you do not wish to place a bid in this event, click on the blue **"I don't wish to participate"** button.
   [image: "I don't wish to participate" button]

> **Note.** The below Dialogue box opens.

2. Select a reason for not participating from the below options.
   [image: dialogue box with reasons for not participating]
3. Confirm your action by clicking on "I will not participate".
   [image: "I will not participate" confirmation button]

> **Note.** At the bottom of the screen the dialogue box now shows that "You've decided to not participate, Want to change your response?"

[image: bottom bar reading "You've decided to not participate, Want to change your response?"]

> **Note.** You can undo this action at anytime by clicking on "I will participate".
