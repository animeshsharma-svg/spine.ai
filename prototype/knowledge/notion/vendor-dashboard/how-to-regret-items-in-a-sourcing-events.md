# How to Regret Items in a sourcing events?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Regret Items in a sourcing events? (https://app.notion.com/p/f5892226c8b282679cd4814d4e1f350f) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Vendor Dashboard

The **Regret Item** feature allows vendors to inform the buyer that they will not be submitting a bid for a specific line item in an event. This is typically used when the vendor is unable to supply the item due to reasons such as:

- The item is not in stock
- The item is not part of the vendor's product catalog
- The vendor is unable to provide the item at this time

By using this feature, vendors can clearly indicate non-participation at the item level while still participating in the rest of the event. This ensures transparency for the buyer and reduces the need for manual clarification or follow-ups.

## Steps — How to Regret Items in a sourcing event

In the Technical Stage Event Tab…

> **Note.** This is the Technical Stage screen, here you fill Vendor Assessments and Line Item Specifications.

[image: Technical Stage screen]

1. Click on the ⚡ Quick Fill option.
2. You can regret items by pressing on **'Regret Item'**, which means that you will not be placing a bid for this item.
3. All Vendor Responses for that item will be written as "Regret".
4. These line items will be crossed out in the technical stage, all future stages (RFQ and RA) and in the QCS for the buyer.
5. You will not be able to place a commercial bid for the item.
