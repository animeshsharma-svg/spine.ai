# How do I add Subsidiaries as a Broker?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I add Subsidiaries as a Broker? (https://app.notion.com/p/2f192226c8b2823ba22581b474102507) · last edited 2026-04-20
Section: Vendor Dashboard

As a broker on the Procol platform, you can manage multiple suppliers and onboard new ones directly from your vendor dashboard. This allows you to bid on behalf of suppliers during events and streamline supplier participation.

## Step-by-Step Process — Add Subsidiaries as a Broker

1. Log in to your **Vendor/Supplier Dashboard**.
   [image: Vendor/Supplier Dashboard]
2. On the top-right corner, click on your **Profile** icon.
   [image: Profile icon]
3. Hover over your profile to see three options:
   - Manage Suppliers
   - My Profile
   - Logout
   [image: profile menu with three options]
4. Click on **Manage Suppliers**.
   [image: Manage Suppliers option]
5. On the **Manage Suppliers** page, you can view all suppliers that you have already onboarded.
   [image: Manage Suppliers page]
6. To add a new supplier, click on the **Add Supplier** button at the top-right.
   [image: Add Supplier button]
7. A supplier creation form will appear. Fill in the following details:
   [image: supplier creation form]
   - **Full Name** – Type the supplier's name.
   - **Phone Number** – Select the country code and enter the phone number.
   - **Email Address** – Enter the supplier's email.
   - **Business Location** – Select a country from the searchable dropdown.
8. Based on the selected business location, a **Tax ID field** will automatically appear:
   - India → GST
   - Estonia → VAT
   - Other countries → respective Tax ID
9. Enter the **Tax ID** to proceed.
10. If a supplier does not have a Tax ID, click the **blue highlighted text** under the field. This will:
    - Hide the Business Location and Tax ID fields.
    - Allow you to manually enter:
      - Company Name
      - Company Address
      - City (selectable via searchable dropdown)
11. If you want to return to using the Tax ID option, click the **blue highlighted text** again.
12. Once all fields are filled, click **Done**.
13. A tooltip will appear confirming **"Vendor Created."**

> **Note.** You will be redirected back to the **Suppliers List**, where the new supplier will now be visible. You can now bid on behalf of any supplier in future events.

[image: Suppliers List showing the new supplier]
