# How to participate in Events as a Vendor?

## Steps to Participate in Events — Participate in Events as a Vendor

> **Note.** When an Event is live, this is what the Vendor sees.

[image: vendor dashboard with a live Event card]

1. Click on the Event Card.
   [image: Event card]

> **Note.** This is the Event Overview screen that opens after clicking on the Event card.

[image: Event Overview screen]

> **Note.** This is an important choice to make that informs the buyer your willingness to participate in this event.

[image: participation choice on the Event Overview screen]

2. If you wish to continue, click on the blue **"I will participate"** button.
   [image: "I will participate" button]
3. If not automatically taken to the next tab, click on the tab next to "Event Overview".
   [image: tab next to Event Overview]

> **Note.** This is how the screen would look. This is the onboarding form that you need to fill and submit. Scroll through the various sections and be sure to fill all the Mandatory fields.

4. Fill the Quote sheet.
   [image: Quote sheet]
5. After filling in the form, scroll to the bottom and click on "Submit".
   [image: Submit button at the bottom of the form]

> **Note.** You will get a confirmation at the top of the screen.

[image: confirmation shown at the top of the screen]

> **Note.** You have successfully submitted your response! Now we wait for evaluation to be done by the buyer.

[image: submitted response view]

> **Note.** If the submission time is still running, you can still make changes and re-submit your response.

[image: re-submit option while submission time is running]
