# How to Use Smart Awarding for AI-Powered Bid Selection

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Use Smart Awarding for AI-Powered Bid Selection (https://app.notion.com/p/84c92226c8b2838697b7010b62deadc8) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: AI Features

**Description:**
Smart Awarding is an AI-powered tool that helps buyers decide which vendors to award and how to distribute quantities post-event. By analyzing both technical and commercial parameters, Smart Awarding significantly reduces manual effort while ensuring data-backed decision-making. It is available after event closure on the Master QCS page of any RFx.

**Persona:**
**Buyers / Procurement Leads / Technical Evaluators** – Users responsible for selecting vendors post-evaluation or making awarding recommendations based on a combination of price, technical scores, and business logic.

## Step-by-Step Process — Smart Awarding

1. Open any completed RFx event and navigate to the **"Master QCS"** page.
   [image: Master QCS page]
2. Click on either **"Select Awarding"** or **"Bid Selection Proposal Draft"** to proceed to the awarding screen. The Awarding Screen opens up.
   [image: Select Awarding / Bid Selection Proposal Draft buttons]
   [image: awarding screen]
3. On the awarding screen, locate and click on the **"Smart Awarding"** button at the top right (next to the **"Create Proposal"** button).
4. A side panel opens on the right with a **chat interface** powered by Procol AI.
5. You now have two options to get started:
   - Choose from one of the **recommended awarding prompts** shown in the panel
   - Or, manually **type a custom awarding rule or logic** in the text box
6. You can also select an **awarding mode** from within the input:
   - **Balanced** – Considers both technical score and commercial savings equally
   - **Technical** – Prioritizes technical score
   - **Commercial** – Focuses on cost savings
7. Select a recommendation like **"Best Value Vendor Selection"** which auto-fills the chat with a prompt designed to award vendors based on the best value per item considering both price and technical score.
8. Click the **blue send icon** to submit the prompt to the AI.
9. The system will take a few seconds to process the request and generate a suggested awarding plan.
10. Review the **AI response**, which includes:
    - Suggested vendor(s) to award
    - Justification for each awarding decision
    - Quantity allocation logic, if applicable
    [image: AI awarding response]
11. You can use the AI's suggestion directly or make manual adjustments based on your judgment or organizational policies.
    [image: awarding screen with AI suggestion applied]
12. Once you are satisfied, click on the **"Create Proposal"** button to proceed with finalizing the award.

> **Note.** Your proposal will now reflect the decision made, and awarding is completed in a significantly faster, intelligent manner.
