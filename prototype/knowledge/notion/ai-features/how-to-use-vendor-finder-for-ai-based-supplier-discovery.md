# How to Use Vendor Finder for AI-Based Supplier Discovery

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Use Vendor Finder for AI-Based Supplier Discovery (https://app.notion.com/p/a1092226c8b282e9be11812be0936d3c) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: AI Features

**Description:**
Vendor Finder is an AI-powered discovery tool within the vendor onboarding module that helps procurement teams scout, identify, and invite new suppliers directly from within the platform. This smart feature reduces the time and effort needed to manually search for vendors by using AI to scan across the internet and recommend relevant suppliers based on product and location details.

**Persona:**
**Buyers / Vendor Managers / Supplier Development Teams** – Anyone responsible for finding new, relevant vendors and onboarding them into the procurement system efficiently.

## Step-by-Step Process — Vendor Finder

1. Navigate to the **"Vendors"** tab using the left-side navigation panel on the Procol platform.
   [image: Vendors tab]
2. On the top right of the page, locate and click on the **"✨ Vendor Finder"** button (next to the **"Start Onboarding"** button).
   [image: Vendor Finder button]
3. A side panel will open, titled "**Smart Supplier Scout**", which allows you to begin vendor discovery using AI.
   [image: Smart Supplier Scout side panel]
4. You now have two options for starting your search:
   - Use one of the **ready-made prompt templates** that suggest vendor discovery queries based on product and location.
   - Or, manually **type a custom prompt** in the textbox describing the kind of vendor you are looking for.
5. Once your prompt is ready, click the **blue search icon** on the right side of the textbox.
6. The system will take a few moments to scan across online sources and databases and then display a **list of vendor cards** based on your query.
   [image: list of vendor cards]
7. Some vendor cards may be marked with a **"Recommended"** tag, indicating high relevance and availability of complete information.
8. Each vendor card shows:
   - Vendor name
   - Location
   - Email (if available)
   - Reference/source link
   - Status flag (e.g., "email not found" if unavailable)
   [image: vendor card fields]
9. To move forward, click on the **"Select"** button on the vendor card for any supplier you wish to invite.
10. You can **select multiple suppliers**, and once you're ready, click on the **"Invite All Selected"** button located at the bottom right of the panel.
11. A confirmation message will appear stating that the **invitation has been successfully sent** to the selected vendors.
    [image: invitation sent confirmation]
12. These vendors are now treated as **prospective suppliers** in your system and can proceed through the regular onboarding workflow.
