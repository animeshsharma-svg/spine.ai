# How Vendors Review and Update Charges After Lot Splitting

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How Vendors Review and Update Charges After Lot Splitting (https://app.notion.com/p/78592226c8b2829a866601d305d97d35) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFQ

Once line items are split into lots during the evaluation stage of an RFQ, vendors must review and revise their quotes, especially other charges like freight, packaging, or handling, which may vary based on the new distribution of line items.

## Persona — Vendors Review Charges After Lot Splitting

Vendor participating in an RFQ event where line items have been split into multiple lots.

## Steps for Vendors to Review Lot Adjustment and Update Charges — Vendors Review Charges After Lot Splitting

1. Navigate to the **Live Events** tab from your supplier dashboard.
2. You will see a **"Bid Adjustment Pending"** tag above your event card—this indicates that the RFQ has been restructured into multiple lots, and your existing charges may no longer be valid.
3. Click into the specific **Lot RFQ** you wish to update.
4. At the top of the lot page, a banner will appear prompting you to **Review Lot Adjustment and Update Charges**.
5. Click on **View Item Distribution** to see how the original line items have been reassigned into different lots.
6. Return to the lot RFQ screen and scroll to the bottom of the main table.
7. Under the **Other Charges** section (freight, packaging, etc.), update your charges as needed based on the revised item distribution.
8. Once the updates are complete, click **Revise Quote** to submit the revised lot-specific quotation.

> **Note.** In some cases, buyers may choose to skip vendor review of lot adjustments. In those cases, this workflow may not apply.

> **Note.** You have successfully reviewed your bids after Lot Splitting. The Banner has disappeared.
