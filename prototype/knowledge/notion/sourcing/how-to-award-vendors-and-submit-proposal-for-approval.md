# How to Award Vendors and Submit Proposal for Approval

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Award Vendors and Submit Proposal for Approval (https://app.notion.com/p/d0892226c8b282cb85be01dc6eac7de2) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › Negotiation and Awarding

After negotiations are complete in RFQ or auction stages, buyers can proceed to award vendors and submit proposals for internal approval using the Master QCS (MQCS) and award module. This guide outlines the step-by-step process to ensure seamless awarding and internal compliance.

## Persona — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

Buyers involved in evaluating and awarding vendors post-commercial negotiation stages.

## Step-by-Step Process — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

### 1. Accessing the Master QCS — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

- Navigate to your **latest commercial stage** (RFQ or auction).
  [image: latest commercial stage]
- Click on the **Master QCS** tab to access the evaluation summary.
  [image: Master QCS tab]
- The MQCS provides:
  - Final and initial price comparisons
  - Technical and commercial rankings
  - Custom columns as per client configurations
  - Visibility of charges across different stages
  [image: Master QCS evaluation summary]

### 2. Starting the Awarding Process — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

- Click on the **"Start Awarding"** button on the top right.
  [image: "Start Awarding" button]
- You will be redirected to the awarding screen with:
  - Total selected line items
  - Proposal value
  - L1 proposal value
  - Non-L1 proposal value
  [image: awarding screen summary]

### 3. Awarding Line Items to Vendors — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

[image: awarding screen line items]

- For each vendor under each line item, select the checkbox to initiate awarding.
  [image: award checkbox]
- A dialog will appear:
  - Confirm awarding
  - Modify the quantity to award if needed
  - Click **"Award"** to confirm.
  [image: award dialog]

> **Note.** You may compare global charges and other costs between different commercial stages by scrolling to the bottom of the awarding screen. [image: global charges comparison at the bottom of the awarding screen]

### 4. Creating and Reviewing the Proposal — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

- Once awarding is completed, click **"Create Proposal"** on the top right.
  [image: "Create Proposal" button]
- A **Note for Approval** screen will open:
  - Fill out any required additional information.
  - Review:
    - Event overview
    - Allocation summary
    - Comparison sheets
    - List of participants and allocations
  [image: Note for Approval screen sections (4 screenshots)]

### 5. Submitting the Proposal for Approval — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

- Click **"Submit Proposal"** on the top right.
  [image: "Submit Proposal" button]
- A dialog will prompt you to:
  - Select the appropriate approval workflow
  - Review the proposal details (line items, values, type)
  - Add closing remarks if needed
  [image: submit proposal dialog]
- Scroll down to verify the approval chain.
  [image: approval chain]
- Click **"Submit Proposal"** on the bottom right.
  [image: "Submit Proposal" button at bottom right]

### 6. Approval Workflow — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

- You'll be taken to the **Approval Screen**, where:
  [image: Approval Screen]
  - The generated note for approval is visible
  - Event overview and allocation details are displayed
  - The full approval chain is listed on the right
- If you're an approver, you can take action to **Approve** or **Reject** the proposal.
  [image: Approve / Reject actions]

## Finding the Approval as an Approver — Award Vendors and Submit Proposal

As an Approver you can find the Approval under the approvers tab

1. Navigate to the **"Approvals"** tab in the left navigation panel.
   [image: Approvals tab in the left navigation]

> **Note.** This is the approvals page. [image: approvals page]

2. Click on the Event that you would like to view for Approval

> **Note.** This is the Approvals/Proposals page. [image: Approvals/Proposals page]

3. You can review the NFA and Proposal and leave your remarks. Then you can approve or reject accordingly.
   [image: NFA review with remarks and approve/reject]

> **Note.** Once all the approvals have been received the system automatically sends a request to the ERP to generate a purchase order. [image: approval completed]

4. You can view the purchase order that has been generated in the "**Purchase Orders" Tab**
   [image: Purchase Orders tab]

> **Note.** This is how the Purchase orders page looks like. [image: Purchase Orders page]

5. Here you can see the Purchase orders that have been requested from the Event just now awarded by us
   [image: purchase orders requested from the awarded event]

> **Note.** Here we have successfully awarded to the vendors and the purchase order have been requested for generation.
