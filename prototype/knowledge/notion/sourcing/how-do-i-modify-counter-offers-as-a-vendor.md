# How do I Modify Counter Offers as a Vendor?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I Modify Counter Offers as a Vendor? (https://app.notion.com/p/3c692226c8b283388d1181edcde2deb2) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › Negotiation and Awarding

When a buyer sends a counter offer, vendors can choose to accept, reject, or modify it. This guide covers how vendors can submit a modified bid—also known as a re-counter—if they do not fully agree with the original counter offer.

## Persona — Modify Counter Offers as a Vendor

Vendor

## Steps to Modify a Counter Offer — Modify Counter Offers as a Vendor

1. Open the RFQ event where you have received a counter offer from the buyer.
   [image: RFQ event with a counter offer received]
2. Observe the Counter Offer Banner
   *This banner appears above the line item or vendor quote sheet*
   [image: Counter Offer Banner]
3. Click on the counter offer - Decline option
   *The below Dialogue Box opens for Declining the Counter offer*
   [image: Decline option on the counter offer]
   [image: dialogue box for declining the counter offer]
4. If you do not agree with the offer, choose the **"Modify Counter Offer"** option instead of rejecting it.
   *The system prompts you to either decline or modify the offer, allowing flexibility in negotiation.*
   *The Below banner changes to this.*
   [image: "Modify Counter Offer" option]
   [image: banner after choosing Modify Counter Offer]
5. Enter your updated value in the editable field.
   *Example: If the original price was ₹90 and the buyer offered ₹80, you can counter it with ₹85.*
   [image: editable field with updated value]
6. Once done click on the "Place Modified Bid" blue button
   [image: "Place Modified Bid" button]
7. Add a remark explaining the reason for your modification.
   *This helps buyers understand your pricing rationale and improves the chances of approval.*
   [image: remark field]
8. Click the blue **"Place Modified Bid"** button to confirm and submit your modified counter offer.
   [image: confirm "Place Modified Bid"]

> **Note.** The system updates the QCS for the buyer, resolving the counter offer action. *Your tag will now display **"Modified Counter Offer Bid"** with an orange thumbs-up icon.* [image: "Modified Counter Offer Bid" tag]
