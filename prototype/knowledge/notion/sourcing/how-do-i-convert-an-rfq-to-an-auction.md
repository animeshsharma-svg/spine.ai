# How do I convert an RFQ to an Auction

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I convert an RFQ to an Auction (https://app.notion.com/p/25892226c8b28383b1b9017e05f640ad) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFQ

**Persona:** Buyer

This guide explains how a buyer can convert an RFQ event into an auction event within the same project, in order to make the bidding process more competitive and leverage additional auction strategies.

## Why Convert an RFQ to Auction — Convert an RFQ to an Auction

After concluding your RFQ event, you may want to continue negotiations by converting the event into an auction. This allows you to retain the same vendors, line items, and previously submitted quotations. The final quotations from the RFQ can be carried forward as starting bids in the auction. This feature is particularly useful for encouraging better pricing through auction strategies that are not available in standard RFQ events.

## Steps to Convert an RFQ to an Auction — Convert an RFQ to an Auction

After concluding your RFQ event, you may choose to convert it into an auction. This allows you to retain the same vendors, line items, and previously submitted quotations. The final quotations from the RFQ can be carried forward as starting bids in the auction to drive competitive pricing.

1. To begin, go to the **Settings** tab within the RFQ event and click on **Convert to Auction**.
2. A prompt will appear asking whether to use the initial or final RFQ quotations as starting bids in the auction.
   1. Leave the checkbox selected to use the RFQ quotations.
   2. Uncheck it if you want the auction to start with fresh bids from vendors.
3. Upon proceeding, you will be redirected to the Auction Setup screen. On this screen, certain aspects will be locked:
   - You cannot change the event template.
   - You cannot modify the products or quantities.
   - You can still update the list of vendors invited.
4. A summary will show how many vendors submitted quotes in the RFQ. You can click on **View All RFQs** to return to the RFQ and review existing bids.
5. Set your auction timings and configure available auction settings. Note that some auction types, such as **Knockout** and **Dutch Auctions**, cannot be selected during conversion as they do not support pre-existing quotations.
6. You can enable optional features like cost breakdown mode, dynamic vendor selection, and choose between line-wise or lot-wise bidding.

> **Note.** To learn more about Cost Breakdown Mode, see the related Knowledge Base article.

7. Once configuration is complete, click **Save**.
8. After saving, click **Publish** to review your auction details and finalize scheduling. Click **Publish** again to make the auction live.

> **Note.** The auction is now added to the same project.

> **Note.** Vendors who submitted bids in the RFQ will be automatically enrolled and can begin participating in the auction with their previous quotations already considered.
