# How to Split Line Items into Lots in an RFQ Event

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Split Line Items into Lots in an RFQ Event (https://app.notion.com/p/ba892226c8b28318a9628152ba72f8f8) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFQ

Lot Splitting allows you to divide a single RFQ event into multiple lots based on line items. This is useful when different groups of items can be awarded independently, enabling separate evaluations and PO generations.

## Persona — Split Line Items into Lots

Buyer or Procurement Manager responsible for structuring RFQ events and awarding line items to vendors.

## Steps to Split Line Items into Lots — Split Line Items into Lots

1. Go to your RFQ Event and navigate to the **Settings** tab.
2. In the dropdown menu, select **Split Line Items into Lots**.
3. A side panel will open titled **Manage Lots**. By default, all line items are in **Lot 1**.

To assign line items to different lots:

4. Click the dropdown next to the line item serial number.
5. Select a different lot number (up to a maximum of 4 lots).
6. The line item will automatically shift to the selected lot.
7. Repeat the above step until all line items are grouped into their respective lots.
8. After finalizing lot configurations, go to the lot header and click the **Publish Lot** button.

> **Note.** You can only publish lots during the evaluation stage of the RFQ. This option is not available during the submission window.

9. Once done you can click on close in the bottom right.
10. Once published, a separate **Master QCS** tab will be created for each lot, and the original unified Master QCS will no longer be visible.

> **Note.** Tip: Ensure that lots are created such that each can be evaluated and awarded independently to prevent conflicts in awarding.

> **Note.** You've successfully split your RFQ into Lots.

## After the split — Split Line Items into Lots

Once the Lots are split, the vendors are prompted to adjust their global / Other charges based on the lot split. Only after vendors do this can you go ahead with any other operations.

> **Note.** See how vendors can adjust and review lot splits in "How Vendors Review and Update Charges After Lot Splitting".

> **Note.** There is also an option to Skip Vendor Review. See "How to Skip Vendor Review in Lot Splitting".
