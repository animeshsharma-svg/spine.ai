# How do I use Request for Best Offer?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I use Request for Best Offer? (https://app.notion.com/p/96f92226c8b2830e9e6b017055821d7b) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFQ

This document guides you through the process of initiating a **Request for Best Offer** during the RFQ evaluation period. This feature enables buyers to invite vendors to revise and resubmit their most competitive quotations, improving the chances of achieving optimal pricing outcomes before final award decisions are made.

**User Persona:**
**Buyer** or **Procurement Manager** involved in sourcing activities and vendor evaluation. This user analyzes bids, negotiates with vendors, and is responsible for awarding the best possible deal for their organization.

## Steps to Request Best Offer from Vendors — Request for Best Offer

1. **Navigate to the RFQ Settings Tab**
   - Ensure the RFQ event is in the **Evaluation** stage before proceeding.
   - Open the event card from the **Events** tab and click on the **Settings** section on the left panel.
2. **Click on "Request for Best Offer"**
   - Click on the **Settings** section on the left panel, and then "Request for Best Offer".
3. A right-hand side panel will appear, allowing you to configure the Best Offer request.
   1. **Set the Duration for Best Offer Submission**
      - Enter the amount of time vendors will get to revise and submit their final offers.
      - Note: This can be set in minutes, hours, or days based on the urgency or project deadline.
   2. **Select the Vendors to Invite**
      - You can invite **all vendors** or choose specific vendors based on their initial bid quality or potential.
      - This gives you the flexibility to focus on the most competitive or strategic vendors.
4. **Click "Send Request" to Launch the Best Offer Phase**
   - Once sent, vendors will receive system notifications and emails prompting them to respond.
5. **Track System Tags and Updates**
   - After the request is sent:
     - The event is tagged as **Best Offer Requested**.
     - Each vendor's bid card or row will also reflect this status.
     - You'll see real-time updates as vendors start submitting new quotations.
6. **Vendor Interface Experience**
   - Vendors see a prominent banner/notification requesting them to submit their **Best Offer**.
   - They can revise applicable line items directly during the configured window.
7. **View Updated Submissions in Supplier's Best Offer Section**
   - Once submissions are received, you can:
     - Review the revised prices and fields in the **comparison view**.
     - Validate whether the vendor has improved their offer over the previous bid.
     - Continue with another best offer request or proceed to awarding as required.
