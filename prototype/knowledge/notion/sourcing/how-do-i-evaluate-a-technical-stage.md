# How do I Evaluate a Technical Stage?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I Evaluate a Technical Stage? (https://app.notion.com/p/2a492226c8b282d0b9e681eaa9204a55) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFP / Technical Stage

This guide is intended for **Technical Evaluators** responsible for reviewing vendor submissions and assigning technical scores in a sourcing event on the Procol platform.

## Finding the event — Evaluate a Technical Stage

1. After logging into the Procol platform, navigate to the **Events** page.
   [image: Events page]
2. Locate the relevant event. Events requiring your evaluation will be clearly marked using a **status widget** on the event card as "Evaluation Needed"
   [image: Evaluation Needed status widget]
3. Click on the event to open the **Project Details** page.
   [image: Project Details page]

## Evaluating vendors — Evaluate a Technical Stage

4. From the **Project Details** page, click on the **Technical Stage** tab.
   Here, you can:
   - View vendor submissions
   - Review specification details
   - Begin scoring vendors
   [image: Technical Stage tab]
5. Each submission will have an **evaluation section** with a **plus icon**.
   - Click **"Add Evaluation"** to begin.
   - You can evaluate at either:
     - **Line item level**
     - **Lot level** (entire vendor)
     As set per the buyer.
   [image: Add Evaluation]
6. There are multiple Technical Ranks that can be assigned to the Line Item
   - **Binary Evaluation**:
     - *Technically Accepted (TA)*
     - *Technically Not Accepted (DNA)*
   or…
   [image: binary evaluation]
   - **Ranked Evaluation**:
     - Assign technical ranks such as *T1, T2, T3*, etc.

> **Note.** Remarks are mandatory when submitting an evaluation.

[image: remarks]

7. After selecting the rank and adding your remark, click **"OK"** to confirm the evaluation.
   [image: OK]

> **Note.** Repeat this for all relevant vendors and line items.

[image: evaluations completed]

> **Note.** **Specification Changes**: Any changes made by the vendor are **highlighted in yellow** within the QCS (Quotation Comparison Sheet) for easy identification.
> **Regretted Line Items**: Line items that vendors have chosen to regret are clearly marked. This indicates the vendor will not supply those items.
> [image: regretted line items]

## Submitting the score — Evaluate a Technical Stage

8. Once all evaluations are completed, click on the **"Submit Score"** button (blue, top right).
   [image: Submit Score]
9. You will be prompted to:
   - **Select an Approval Workflow** based on conditions such as number of DNA vendors or line items.
   - **Click Next** to proceed.
   [image: approval workflow selection]
10. A dialog will open for you to:
    - Enter your **closing remarks**
    - Review the selected **approval workflow**
    [image: closing remarks dialog]
11. Click the **"Submit"** button to confirm and send it for approval.
    [image: Submit]
12. After submission, you'll be taken to the **Approval Page** where:
    - The workflow progress is displayed.
    - No further action is required from your side until the approval is completed.
    [image: Approval Page]
