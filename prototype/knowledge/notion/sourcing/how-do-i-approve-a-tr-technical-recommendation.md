# How do I approve a TR (Technical Recommendation)?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I approve a TR (Technical Recommendation)? (https://app.notion.com/p/72e92226c8b28215a1f4814528a262e4) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFP / Technical Stage

Technical recommendation proposals are created once a technical evaluator has completed evaluating vendors or line items during the **Technical Stage** of an event. These proposals are then sent for approval to designated authorities—such as Department Heads, Procurement Managers, or CXOs—before the rankings become visible to the buyer.

This ensures that technical evaluations are reviewed and verified by senior officials before they influence any commercial decision-making.

## When Are Technical Approvals Required? — Approve a TR (Technical Recommendation)

- When technical rankings (e.g., T1, T2, TNA,TA etc.) are assigned at **line item** or **lot level**.
- Before buyers can view technical evaluations.
- To confirm the correctness and completeness of the evaluation process.

A proposal can be **rejected**, in which case the evaluator can revise and **resend it for approval**.

## How to Approve a Technical Recommendation — Approve a TR (Technical Recommendation)

1. **Open the Approvals Module**
   Navigate to the **Approvals** tab from the left-hand panel.
   [image: Approvals tab]
2. Click on **"Pending on Me"** to view all approvals awaiting your action.
   [image: Pending on Me]
3. Locate the relevant approval entry.
4. Click on the **arrow icon** to open the proposal and access the linked event.
5. On the proposal page, you can see the following:
   [image: approval entry arrow icon]
   - **TR Status**
   - **Project Reference ID**
   - **Technical Ranks**
   - **Approval Workflow Stages**
   - **Comments (Left by earlier approvers or the technical evaluator)**
   - **Technical Specification**
   [image: proposal page]
6. After reviewing, Enter your **remarks** in the comment box.
7. Click on the **"Approve"** button to approve.
   Or click on **"Reject"** if changes are needed.
   [image: Approve / Reject]
8. If approved, the proposal moves to the **next approver** in the workflow.
   [image: next approver]
9. Once **all approvers** have approved, the proposal is marked as approved.
   [image: proposal approved]

> **Note.** The buyer can now **see the technical rankings** in the event interface.
> [image: technical rankings visible to buyer]
