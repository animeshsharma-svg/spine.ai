# How do Re-Evaluate Line items in Technical Stage?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do Re-Evaluate Line items in Technical Stage? (https://app.notion.com/p/4bf92226c8b28358a41801bb13d445f3) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFP / Technical Stage

**Description**

When a buyer requests re-evaluation on certain line items, technical evaluators are notified and must act on the request. This guide explains how to accept or reject re-evaluation and what happens in each case.

## Persona — Re-Evaluate Line items in Technical Stage

Technical Evaluator

## Steps to Respond to a Re-Evaluation Request — Re-Evaluate Line items in Technical Stage

1. Navigate to the sourcing event and open the Technical Stage.
   [image: Technical Stage]
2. Identify line items marked as "Re-evaluation Requested."
   [image: Re-evaluation Requested line items]
3. Review the reason for the re-evaluation and the buyer who raised the request.
   *The original technical scores and recommendations will also be visible for reference.*
   [image: re-evaluation reason]
4. Choose to either **Accept** or **Reject** the re-evaluation request.
   [image: Accept / Reject]
5. If you choose **Accept**, a confirmation dialog will appear showing the selected line items.
   *Click on the "Accept and Re-evaluate" button.*
   [image: Accept and Re-evaluate]
6. Modify the technical scores and recommendations as needed.
   *You can reassign rankings (T1, T2, etc.) and update individual scores.*
   [image: modify scores]
7. Click on the Submit button under the TR status widget
   [image: Submit under TR status widget]
8. Add closing remarks in the remarks section.
9. If approval workflows are enabled, select the appropriate approval flow.
10. Click the **Submit** button to finalize the updated evaluation.
    [image: final Submit]

> **Note.** You have successfully Re-evaluated the Line Items!
> [image: re-evaluation complete]

## If you choose Reject — Re-Evaluate Line items in Technical Stage

> **Note.** If you choose **Reject**, a dialog box will appear listing the line items being rejected.
> [image: reject dialog]

1. Enter a mandatory rejection reason in the textbox.
   [image: rejection reason]
2. Click on **Reject Re-evaluation** once the button turns blue.
   *The technical rankings will remain unchanged and the status will return to "TR Submitted."*
   [image: Reject Re-evaluation]
   [image: status TR Submitted]
