# How to Send and Manage Counter Offers?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Send and Manage Counter Offers? (https://app.notion.com/p/bec92226c8b282d08ba081dbd57b4ba2) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › Negotiation and Awarding

Counter offers are a key negotiation tool in the procurement process. Buyers can send counter offers on price, quantity, or other fields to vendors. Vendors can respond by accepting, rejecting, or modifying these offers. This guide explains how to send, view, and manage counter offers from the buyer's side.

**Persona**

Buyer

## Steps to Send and Manage Counter Offers — Send and Manage Counter Offers

1. Navigate to the RFQ tab of your sourcing event or project.
2. Open the QCS (Quote Comparison Sheet) to view vendor bids.
   [image: QCS with vendor bids]
3. Identify the vendor you want to send a counter offer to.
   *Each vendor row will display a blue "Counter Offer" button beneath their name.*
4. Click the **"Counter Offer"** button to open the counter offer dialog box.
   [image: "Counter Offer" button beneath the vendor name]
   [image: counter offer dialog box]
5. Select the field you want to apply a counter offer on.
   *Common fields include Price and Quantity. You may also send counter offers on global fields.*
   [image: field selection in the counter offer dialog]
6. Input the new value you want to propose.
   *The dialog will show both the original vendor value and the counter offer value for easy comparison.*
   [image: original vendor value vs counter offer value]

> **Note.** For partial awarding, update quantities field with adjusted values. *This allows for partial awarding per line item, if needed.* [image: quantity field adjusted for partial awarding]

> **Note.** You can also send Counter Offers on Global Fields (fields that are not specific to any particular line item). [image: global fields in the counter offer dialog]

7. (Optional) Add a note to the vendor explaining the counter offer.
   *This is helpful for negotiation context but not mandatory.*
   [image: note to vendor field]
8. Click **"Send Counter Offer"** to confirm.
   *You will receive a tooltip notification that the counter offer was sent successfully.*
   [image: "Send Counter Offer" button]
   [image: counter offer sent confirmation]
9. After sending, a tag saying **"Counter Offer Requested"** appears above the vendor's name in the QCS.
   *You will also see a "Revoke Counter Offer" option.*
   *Changed values are highlighted, showing original vs counter offer values.*
   [image: "Counter Offer Requested" tag in the QCS]

## Additional Actions on Counter Offers — Send and Manage Counter Offers

1. To revoke a counter offer, click **"Revoke Counter Offer"** under the vendor's name.
   *A tooltip will confirm the counter offer has been revoked.*
   [image: "Revoke Counter Offer" option and revoke confirmation]
2. Monitor the vendor's response. Vendors can:
   [image: vendor view of the counter offer]
   - **Accept** the counter offer: A tag will read *"Accepted Counter Offer Bid"* with a green thumbs up icon.
     [image: "Accepted Counter Offer Bid" tag]
   - **Reject** the counter offer: A tag will read *"Last Counter Offer Rejected"*.
     [image: "Last Counter Offer Rejected" tag]
   - **Modify** the counter offer: A tag will read *"Modified Counter Offer Bid"* with an orange thumbs up icon.
     *For example, if your counter offer was ₹80 (original bid ₹90), a vendor might submit ₹85 and provide a remark explaining the adjustment.*
     [image: "Modified Counter Offer Bid" tag]
