# How do I Schedule Events and change Event Duration?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I Schedule Events and change Event Duration? (https://app.notion.com/p/e9892226c8b283249ccf819201c93727) · last edited 2026-04-17
Section: Sourcing › Creating Events

This article explains how to schedule events using Procol's event scheduling panel. Users can configure start time, end time, and evaluation time directly from the event creation page. Events can be started immediately or scheduled for a future date and time with full flexibility over the duration and expiration of the event.

## Persona — Schedule Events and change Event Duration

Planners, Buyers, Procurement managers

## Step-by-step process — Schedule Events and change Event Duration

[image: event creation page]

1. While on the event creation page, go to the bottom-right corner and click on the date range label beside the **"Schedule"** option.
2. A side panel will open showing all event time configurations: Start Time, End Time, and Evaluation Time.

**Start Time**

3. Under **Start Time**, click on the drop-down menu below where it says "Start Now".
4. You will see two options:
   - **Start Now**: The event will start immediately at the current date and time shown.
   - **Schedule for Later**: Allows you to choose a future date and time.
5. If you select **Schedule for Later**, click on the date field to open a calendar widget and select your preferred start date.
6. Click on the time field to type in or select a time using the time picker.

**End Time**

7. Under **End Time**, click on the drop-down where it says "Duration".
8. You can choose from preset durations like:
   - 30 minutes
   - 1 hour
   - 4 hours
   - 1 day
9. Alternatively, click on **Custom Duration** to open a dialog box.
10. In the dialog box, enter a numeric value and select the time unit (Minutes, Hours, Days) from the drop-down.
11. Click **Save** to confirm your custom duration.
12. If you prefer to set a specific end time instead of a duration, change the drop-down from **Duration** to **Fixed Time**.
13. Use the date picker to choose the exact date and time you want the event to end.

**Evaluation Time**

14. Under **Evaluation Time**, click on the drop-down named **"Duration"**.
15. You will see two options:
    - Not Applicable
    - Duration
16. Click on **"Duration"** to open the drop-down and configure how long the event remains open for evaluation after it ends. Select from options like:
    - Not Applicable
    - 30 minutes
    - 1 hour
    - 4 hours
    - 1 day
    - Custom Duration
17. If you select **Custom Duration**, a dialog box will appear where you can enter the numeric value and unit of time. In the dialog box, enter a numeric value and select the time unit (Minutes, Hours, Days) from the drop-down.
18. Click **Save** to confirm your custom duration.
19. To keep the evaluation window open indefinitely (no expiry), choose **Not Applicable**.

> **Note.** You've successfully Scheduled your Event.
