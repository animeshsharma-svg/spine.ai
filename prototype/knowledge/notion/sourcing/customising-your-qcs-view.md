# Customising your QCS View

Section: Sourcing › Others

**Description:**
This guide helps you explore different user-friendly options available on the Procol platform to customize how your QCS (Quote Comparison Sheet) appears. These features are designed to make your comparison process more visual, focused, and intuitive based on your preferences.

**User Persona:**
**Buyer**, **Technical Evaluator**, or **Category Manager** who is reviewing vendor quotations and needs a tailored view of the quote comparison sheet for easier analysis and decision-making.

## Steps to Customize Your QCS View — Customising your QCS View

### 1. Switch Between Vertical and Horizontal View — Customising your QCS View

- Click the layout toggle button to switch between vertical and horizontal views for better readability.
  [image: layout toggle button]
  [image: Vertical view] [image: Horizontal view]

### 2. Enable Full Screen Mode — Customising your QCS View

- Click the **full screen icon** to expand the QCS across your display.
  [image: full screen icon]
- Ideal for deep analysis or when working on large monitors during evaluation meetings.
  [image: QCS in full screen]

### 3. Zoom In and Zoom Out Options — Customising your QCS View

- Use the **zoom control** to increase or decrease the text size for easier readability.
  [image: zoom control]

### 4. View By Vendor Mode — Customising your QCS View

- Click **View by Product** to see a dropdown option with **Vendor** and Product. Click on Vendor to see a vendor-specific quote breakdown instead of the comparative table.
  [image: View by Product dropdown]
- Click on **View by Vendor** to see a dedicated quote card for each vendor individually.
  [image: vendor quote cards]
- This is helpful if you want to review each vendor's quote in isolation without cross-comparison.

### 5. Hide Specific Fields — Customising your QCS View

- Use the **Hide Fields** option to declutter the QCS by hiding columns like GST, Quantity, Delivery Terms, etc.
  [image: Hide Fields option] [image: QCS with fields hidden]
- This helps you focus only on the most relevant fields during evaluations.

### 6. Adjust Column Size with the "Auto" Button — Customising your QCS View

- Click on the **Auto** button to resize row height or column width.
- Options include: **Small**, **Medium**, **Large**, and **XL**, depending on the data density and your screen space.
- **ONLY AVAILABLE IN HORIZONTAL VIEW**
  [image: Auto button size options] [image: resized columns]
