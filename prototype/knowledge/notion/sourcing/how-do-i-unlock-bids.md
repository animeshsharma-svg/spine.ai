# How do I Unlock Bids?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I Unlock Bids? (https://app.notion.com/p/47592226c8b283c08d3a019b44032efd) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFQ

**Unlock Bids** is a compliance-focused feature designed to ensure integrity and transparency during procurement evaluations by restricting access to vendor bids until a controlled unlock process is completed. This prevents premature access and minimizes the risk of collusion.

## Purpose — Unlock Bids

The feature enforces a **two-person authentication system**, where one user initiates the unlock request and another user—typically from a different department—receives the authentication OTP. This setup ensures that neither party can view the bids independently, helping maintain fairness between purchasing and finance teams.

## When Can You Unlock Bids? — Unlock Bids

Bids can **only** be unlocked **after the event's submission window has closed**. Attempting to unlock bids before the submission deadline will not be permitted.

## Steps to Unlock Bids — Unlock Bids

1. **Go to the Event.** Navigate to the **"Responses"** tab within the specific event.
2. **Initiate Unlock Process.** Click on the **settings icon** or hover over it. From the dropdown, select **"Unlock Bids"**.
3. **Choose Unlock Option.** A dialog box will appear with two options:
   - **For Myself** – Unlocks bids only for you.
   - **For Everyone** – Unlocks bids for all users with access to the event.
4. **Select OTP Recipient.** Choose the second user (typically from another department) who will receive the **OTP** required to proceed. (This dropdown shows only a specified number of pre-set users.) Click **"Send OTP"**.
5. **OTP Verification.** The selected recipient will receive the OTP via their registered contact details (email/SMS). Once you receive the OTP from them (usually via an offline/secure channel), enter it and click **"Submit OTP"**.

> **Note.** The bids have been unlocked. If the OTP is verified successfully:
> - Bids will be unlocked and visible according to the selected unlock option ("For Myself" or "For Everyone").
> - You can now proceed to view and evaluate the vendor responses.

This safeguard ensures that bid data is accessed only after proper authorization and mutual agreement, supporting compliance and transparency in your procurement process.
