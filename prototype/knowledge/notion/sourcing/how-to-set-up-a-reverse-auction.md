## Who Is This For? — Set up a Reverse Auction

Buyers, Procurement Managers setting up **Reverse Auctions** on Procol

## Overview — Set up a Reverse Auction

When creating an **Auction** **Event** in Procol, buyers must choose an **Auction Strategy**. This determines how vendors see bids, how competition is structured, and how winners are decided.

You can choose from several strategy types based on your sourcing goals — whether you want aggressive price discovery, structured supplier feedback, or fast commitment.

## How to Choose or Change an Auction Strategy — Set up a Reverse Auction

1. On the **Create Event** screen below, select **Auction** as your event type.

> **Note.** By default, the **Event Type** is set to **Rank on Line**, a type of Reverse Auction.

[image: Create Event screen with Auction selected]

2. Click on the **Rank on Line** badge to choose your **Event Type** to open a side drawer with multiple strategy options.
   [image: Rank on Line badge]
   [image: side drawer with auction strategy options]

> **Note.** You can update the strategy before publishing the event, but not after the event goes live.

> **Note.** Learn how to change and set up different auction strategies here → (link to "How to choose and configure Auction Strategies?")

**Choose how you will award (to the vendors) in the event:**

[image: awarding type options]

- "I may partially award the event to multiple vendors" - This runs the Auction at a line item level
- "I'll award the entire lot to a single vendor" - This runs the Auction at a Lot Level.

3. Click on Select Template
   [image: Select Template]
4. Choose a predefined **template** based on your use case.
   [image: template selection]
5. Add **products/items** for which you're seeking bids by clicking on the cell beneath the "Products" column and selecting from the dropdown.
   You can also type in the cell to narrow your search.
   - You can add more products in each subsequent row. (A new row will automatically be added each time)
   - There is a limit of up to 65 line items for this.
   [image: Products column dropdown]
6. Fill in all **mandatory fields** in the template table. Quantities are free text. However, some fields, such as location, will be dropdowns similar to those for products (e.g., quantity, location, etc.).
   [image: template table with mandatory fields]

> **Note.** Shaded out fields that can't be filled in are for the Vendor to fill. Buyer's cannot fill in these fields.

[image: shaded vendor-only fields]

7. The Procol platform suggests Vendors mapped to the products added by you in the above template. Click on the blue "Invite Vendors" shown in the green tip to automatically add them as participants.
   [image: green tip with Invite Vendors]
8. You can search, filter, or add vendors manually as well by clicking on Participants.
   The Below Screen shows up.
   [image: Participants button]
   [image: participants search/filter screen]

> **Note.** Procol auto-generates the event title using product and location names, but you can edit this as needed.

[image: auto-generated event title]

9. Click on the Blue "Publish Event" button
   [image: Publish Event button]
10. In the Pop-up screen, review all the Event details. If everything seems in order, you can click on the blue "Publish" button,
    This will send an email notification to all added participants, inviting them to participate in the event that is now Live.
    *Alternatively, you can press "Edit Schedule" to schedule the event for later*
    [image: publish review pop-up]

> **Note.** **You Have Successfully Published your Auction Event !!**
> [image: published auction event]
