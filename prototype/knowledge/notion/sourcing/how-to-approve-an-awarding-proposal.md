# How to approve an Awarding proposal?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to approve an Awarding proposal? (https://app.notion.com/p/ac092226c8b282be904481b0fc6295de) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › Negotiation and Awarding

After the Awarding and Proposal Creation is done for a sourcing project an approval is triggered for the same. This guide outlines how you can find these approvals and approve them after reviewing the necessary details.

## Steps — Approve an Awarding proposal

1. Navigate to the **"Approvals"** tab in the left navigation panel.
   [image: Approvals tab in the left navigation panel]

> **Note.** This is the approvals page. [image: approvals page]

2. Click on the Event that you would like to view for Approval
   [image: event in the approvals list]

> **Note.** This is the Approvals/Proposals page, here you can review all the details of the sourcing events. The allocations summary, overview, and more. [image: Approvals/Proposals page]

3. You can review the NFA and Proposal and leave your remarks. Then you can approve or reject accordingly.
   [image: NFA and Proposal review with remarks, approve/reject]

> **Note.** Once all the approvals have been received the system automatically sends a request to the ERP to generate a purchase order. [image: approvals received]

4. You can view the purchase order that has been generated in the "**Purchase Orders" Tab**
   [image: Purchase Orders tab]

> **Note.** This is how the Purchase orders page looks like. [image: Purchase Orders page]

5. Here you can see the Purchase orders that have been requested from the Event just now awarded by us
   [image: purchase orders requested from the awarded event]

> **Note.** Here we have successfully approved the awarding proposal and the purchase order has been requested for generation.
