# How to add Terms and Conditions for my Vendors in my Events?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to add Terms and Conditions for my Vendors in my Events? (https://app.notion.com/p/9e892226c8b2826c8a37012c17486ea9) · last edited 2026-04-21
Section: Sourcing › Creating Events

Procol allows you to attach **Terms and Conditions (T&Cs)** to both **projects** and **events**, ensuring vendors formally acknowledge your commercial, legal, or operational expectations before participating. You can add T&Cs in the form of text or uploaded documents.

## Where Can You Add T&Cs? — Terms and Conditions in Events

- **Project Level**: Applies to all events under the project.
- **Event Level**: Applies to specific sourcing stages like RFQs, Auctions, or Technical Rounds.

## Adding Project-Level Terms and Conditions — Terms and Conditions in Events

1. **Go to the Event Creation screen.**
2. Next to the event title, click on the **'Add T&Cs'** button.

A side panel opens where you can:

3. Browse and select from previously used T&Cs under other projects or events.
4. Use checkboxes to include multiple sets of terms.
5. Add your own T&Cs in the text box labeled **'Add your own terms and conditions'**.
6. Click on the plus (+) button when you're ready to add the TnC.
7. Upload files (e.g., images, screenshots, PDFs) using the **Upload** button.
8. You can remove any terms by clicking on the cross (X) next to a T&C.
9. Once done, click **Done** at the bottom right to confirm.

## Adding Event-Specific Terms and Conditions — Terms and Conditions in Events

1. Navigate to the event configuration screen.
2. Look for the **'Edit Terms and Conditions'** button (usually found towards the bottom left).
3. A similar panel opens, allowing you to:
   1. Select from recent or previously used T&Cs.
   2. Manually enter new terms.
   3. You can also **mark all T&Cs as mandatory**, requiring vendors to accept them before submitting bids.
   4. Upload files or documents if needed.
   5. You can remove any terms by clicking on the cross (X) next to a T&C.
   6. Once done, click **Done** at the bottom right to confirm.

> **Note.** You've successfully added Terms and Conditions (T&Cs) to your event.
