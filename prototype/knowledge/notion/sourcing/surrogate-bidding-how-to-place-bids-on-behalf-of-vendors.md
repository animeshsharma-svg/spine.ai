# Surrogate Bidding: How to Place Bids on Behalf of Vendors

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — Surrogate Bidding: How to Place Bids on Behalf of Vendors (https://app.notion.com/p/01692226c8b282c58986816665dc969f) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › Negotiation and Awarding

Surrogate Bidding allows buyers to submit bids on behalf of vendors who are unable to access the Procol vendor platform due to connectivity, availability, or other limitations. This is useful when vendors communicate their bids offline—via phone, email, or other media—and the buyer needs to manually enter their responses.

## Persona — Surrogate Bidding

Buyer - This guide is intended for buyers who are responsible for entering bids and may need to support vendors by placing surrogate bids on their behalf.

## Steps to Place a Surrogate Bid — Surrogate Bidding

1. Navigate to the project details page for the event where you want to place a surrogate bid.
   *This could be a technical evaluation, RFQ, or auction event.*
   [image: project details page of the event]
2. Go to the relevant stage tab — Technical Stage, RFQ, or Reverse Auction — based on where the bid needs to be placed.
   [image: stage tabs (Technical Stage / RFQ / Reverse Auction)]
3. Click on the Participants section to view a list of vendors.
   *Details like name, phone number, email, and vendor code will be visible.*
   [image: Participants section with vendor list]
4. Locate the vendor for whom you want to place a surrogate bid.
   *You can use the search bar to quickly find the vendor.*
   [image: vendor search bar in Participants]
5. Click on the "➕ Add Bid" button next to the vendor's name.
   [image: "Add Bid" button next to the vendor name]
6. Enter the vendor's response in the bid form that opens.
   *You will see the same form the vendor would normally fill out themselves.*
   [image: surrogate bid form]
7. Use Quick Fill to copy-paste or autofill values where applicable.
   *This helps speed up the process for multiple similar entries.*
   [image: Quick Fill option]
8. Add any Supplier Remarks if the vendor has stated specific instructions.
   *For example, "Final price, no further negotiation."*
   [image: Supplier Remarks field]
9. Attach a Surrogate Bid Reference to support the bid.
   *You can upload screenshots, call logs, emails, or any other offline records.*
   [image: Surrogate Bid Reference attachment]
10. Click Submit to place the surrogate bid.
    [image: Submit button and confirmation]
11. Verify that the surrogate bid appears correctly in the QCS.
    *It will be marked with "Surrogate Bid" under the vendor's name in blue text, next to the currency.*
    [image: QCS showing the "Surrogate Bid" marker under the vendor name]

> **Note.** You've successfully placed a Surrogate Bid!
