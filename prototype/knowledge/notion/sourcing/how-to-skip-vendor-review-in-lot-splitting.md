# How to Skip Vendor Review in Lot Splitting

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Skip Vendor Review in Lot Splitting (https://app.notion.com/p/a2092226c8b2837f98fe01359ef8b01d) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFQ

In cases where vendors are unable to revise their global or other charges after lots are split in an RFQ event, buyers have the option to skip vendor review and proceed with the evaluation. This ensures the procurement process continues without unnecessary delays due to vendor unavailability.

## Persona — Skip Vendor Review in Lot Splitting

Buyer managing an RFQ event where line items have been split into multiple lots and vendor input on revised charges is pending.

## Steps to Skip Vendor Review for Lot Adjustment — Skip Vendor Review in Lot Splitting

1. Navigate to the **Lot RFQ** where lot splitting has been completed.
2. Locate the vendor card for which the **Lot Adjustment** is still pending.
3. Click the **"Skip Vendor Review"** button under the vendor's name.
4. A side panel will open displaying the split details for that vendor's lots.
5. Choose how to apply the global charges for the lots:
   - **Pro-rata allocation** based on line item value or quantity, or
   - **Manual entry** of specific global charge values for each lot
6. Enter a valid **reason** for skipping vendor review, such as:
   - Vendor unavailable
   - Vendor facing network issues
   - Vendor not responding
7. Once all required fields are filled, click the **"Confirm & Skip Review"** button.

> **Note.** A confirmation tooltip will confirm that the Vendor review has been skipped!
