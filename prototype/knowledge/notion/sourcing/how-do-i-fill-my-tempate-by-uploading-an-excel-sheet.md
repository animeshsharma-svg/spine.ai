# How do I fill my tempate by uploading an Excel Sheet?

## Step-by-Step Process — Fill template by uploading an Excel Sheet

1. Navigate to your RFQ Event Creation page.
2. Click on the "Upload Sheet" above the RFQ Sheet on the right side.
3. A pop-up module appears for you to upload the excel sheet.
4. Click on "**Download template**" to download a template sheet to fill in the line item details.
5. You can open the sheet that has been downloaded using google sheets or Microsoft Excel.
6. Open the sheet in any supported application.
7. Fill in the sheet with the necessary details.
8. Take a download of the sheet as an .xlsx file.
9. Upload the filled and downloaded file in the modal.
10. Click on OK.

> **Note.** The Product Sheet will now be updated with the filled in details! [image: updated product sheet]
