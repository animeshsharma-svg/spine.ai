# How do I Request for Revaluation?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I Request for Revaluation? (https://app.notion.com/p/71c92226c8b282aa8ed481f38b4148c9) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › RFP / Technical Stage

Buyers may request re-evaluation of technical rankings (T1, T2, T3, TNA) if they find errors or disagree with the initial assessment made by technical evaluators. This guide explains how to initiate the re-evaluation process within a sourcing event.

## Persona — Request for Revaluation

Buyer / Procurement Owner

## Steps to Request Re-Evaluation — Request for Revaluation

1. Navigate to the Technical Stage of the sourcing event.
   [image: Technical Stage]
2. Click on the "Settings" dropdown from the top menu.
3. Select the option "Request for Re-Evaluation."
   *Note: This option is enabled only in the Evaluation Time.*
   [image: Settings dropdown with Request for Re-Evaluation]
4. A side drawer will open labeled "Request Re-Evaluation."
   [image: Request Re-Evaluation drawer]
5. Fill in the reason for re-evaluation in the provided textbox.
   *This field is mandatory and should explain why re-evaluation is needed — e.g., missing information, incorrect evaluation, or vendor clarification.*
   [image: reason textbox]
6. Under "Select Line Items," choose the line items to be re-evaluated.
   *You can select all or specific ones. Only items in the "TR Submitted" stage are eligible.*
   [image: Select Line Items]
7. Scroll to the bottom of the drawer and click on the "Send Request" button.
   *The blue button at the bottom confirms your re-evaluation request.*
   [image: Send Request]

> **Note.** The selected line items will now be marked as "Re-evaluation Requested."
> *This status indicates that the technical evaluators must now take action.*
> [image: Re-evaluation Requested status]

> **Note.** Evaluators will review your request and either accept or reject it.
> *If accepted, rankings will be modified. If rejected, the current ranks remain unchanged.*
> [image: evaluator response]
