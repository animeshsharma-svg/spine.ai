# How do I add additional columns to a Template?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How do I add additional columns to a Template? (https://app.notion.com/p/98e92226c8b28227abc2016fcb9b294b) · last edited 2026-04-21
Section: Sourcing › Creating Events

**Description:**
Procol allows buyers to customize their event line items by adding additional columns for both internal use and vendor-side inputs. These additional fields help capture important parameters such as freight charges, attachments, price caps, and custom instructions. This customization ensures that the event is tailored to specific procurement needs and improves clarity for both buyers and vendors.

**Persona:**
**Buyer / Purchaser** – This user is responsible for configuring event-level details before the RFQ or auction is shared with vendors. They may need to collect extra data points, enforce pricing rules, or allow vendors to upload specific attachments. This feature enables them to fully tailor the bidding structure and requirements for each event.

## Step-by-Step Process — Add additional columns to a Template

1. Navigate to your RFQ Event Creation page.
2. Click on the "+Add Columns" above the RFQ Sheet on the right side.
3. A dropdown appears giving two options:
   - Creator
   - Participant
4. Click on the **"Creator"** option. A dropdown with multiple options appears:
   - **Price Cap** - An upper limit to the price
   - **Creator Attachments** - Attachments that can be uploaded against a line item by the Buyer
   - **Tick Size** - Minimum amount change to be made when editing / revising price quotes
   - **Min Quantity** - Minimum quantity to place bid for
   - **Custom Column** - Any Text column
   - **Tick Size Percentage** - Minimum percentage change to be made when editing / revising price quotes
5. Click on the **"Participant"** option. A dropdown with multiple options appears:
   - **Freight Charges** - Additional charges levied for transportation / freight
   - **Participant Attachments** - Attachments that can be uploaded against the line item by the Vendor
   - **Custom Column** - Any Text column
6. Once a dropdown value is selected it shows up in the RFQ/Auction Sheet. You can now fill in the values in these cells of the newly added column, or the vendor will fill them in if they are participant columns.

> **Note.** The Dropdown value also disappears from its respective list after being added.

> **Note.** You've successfully added the columns.
