# How to Enable Vendor Masking?

Source: Notion Knowledge Base — How to Enable Vendor Masking? (https://app.notion.com/p/d6592226c8b283b28981014976204fd6) · last edited 2026-04-12
Section: Sourcing › Others

Vendor Masking is a key feature that helps eliminate bias and reduce the risk of collusion by hiding vendor identities from buyers during evaluation and negotiation. When enabled, vendors are anonymized (e.g., Vendor 1, Vendor 2, etc.), and buyers can focus solely on bid data.

## Steps to Enable Vendor Masking — Enable Vendor Masking

1. Go to the **RFQ** or **Auction Event** screen where your event is live.
2. Click the **Actions** button to access advanced controls.
   [image: Actions button]
3. Select **Enable Vendor Masking** to hide the names of all the Vendor's names until bidding ends. Names are replaced with Vendor 1,2,3,4….N.
   [image: Enable Vendor Masking option and masked vendor names]

- ***The Participants tab will also be hidden from buyers, preventing them from seeing who has or hasn't participated.***
  [image: event screen with Participants tab hidden]

This ensures that all vendor evaluations are conducted fairly and based solely on the bid values or other evaluation criteria—not on the identity of the vendor.
