## Assigning PRs to your team — Assign PRs to my Team

As a purchase manager, you can assign individual **line items** from a Purchase Requisition (PR) to specific team members, making them responsible for executing those purchases. Once assigned, it becomes their responsibility to create sourcing events and ensure the PR is moved from **Open** to **Completed** status.

### How to Assign a PR — Assign PRs to my Team

1. Hover over the **"+" icon** in the **Assignee** column.
   [image: the "+" icon in the Assignee column]
2. Click on **"Add Assignee"** when the option appears.
   [image: the Add Assignee option]
3. A dropdown will show all available users.
   [image: dropdown of available users]
4. Select the team member you want to assign the line item to.
   [image: a team member selected as assignee]

### Helpful Tip / Best Practices — Assign PRs to my Team

You can assign multiple PR line items at once using **bulk actions**:

1. Select the line items you want to assign by checking the boxes on the left.
   [image: line items selected with checkboxes]
2. Click the **"Add Assignee"** button at the top right.
   [image: the Add Assignee button at the top right]
3. Choose the user you want to assign from the dropdown.
   [image: the assignee dropdown]

> **Note.** This is especially useful when assigning an entire PR or grouping items by category or buyer responsibility.

In both Bulk and Individual Actions, once "Add Assignee" is clicked, a scrollable dropdown is opened where you can select the Assignee. You can also search for a user by typing in the search bar.
