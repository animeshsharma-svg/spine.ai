### Holding PRs — Hold a PR Line Item

If a PR line item needs to be paused or temporarily removed from processing, you can place it on hold.

### How to Hold a Line Item — Hold a PR Line Item

1. Scroll to the far right of the PR table.
2. Click on the **🚫 "Hold Item"** button for the relevant PR line item.
   [image: the Hold Item button on a PR line item row]
3. Enter a **remark** explaining why the item is being held.
   [image: the hold dialog with a remark field]
4. Confirm the action by clicking on the blue **"Hold Item"** button.
5. You can view the Items that you held and who it was held by in the Hold Status Tab of the PR page.
   [image: the Hold status tab showing held items and who held them]

> **Note.** Items can be reopened at any time you want, this action send a signal to your ERP to hold these items there as well.
