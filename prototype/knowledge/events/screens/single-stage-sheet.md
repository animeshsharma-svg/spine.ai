## Test cases written from the single-stage-sheet screen

- GEN-SCR-117: Custom duration without an end time blocks publishing. Steps: Click "Now - 01:23 am, 21st Oct ( 30 Mins )". Choose the custom duration option. Close the duration popover without setting an end time. Click "Publish Event". Expected: Publishing fails and the exact message "Please add end time for the event" appears. [plan: full]
- GEN-SCR-118: Participants opens participant management. Steps: Click "Participants". Click "Invite Vendor". Use "Search vendors you want to add". Expected: The participant area opens, the vendor invitation flow opens, and the vendor search is available for finding vendors to add. [plan: full]
- GEN-SCR-371: Blank event title is rejected. Steps: Leave "Enter the title of this event" blank. Click "Publish Event". Expected: "Enter the title of this event" receives a red outline because the title has an error. The event is not published. [plan: partial]
- GEN-SCR-372: Publish confirmation shows the schedule-edit option. Steps: Click "Publish Event". Do not confirm the publish action. Expected: A confirmation dialog opens with "Publish Event" and the project title truncated to 45 characters. The dialog shows OK text "Publish" and Cancel text "Edit Schedule". [plan: full]
- GEN-SCR-373: Schedule control opens the duration menu. Steps: Click "Now - 04:49 pm, 21st Oct ( 30 Mins )". Expected: A duration menu opens. It includes the default duration choices 30 min, 1 h, 4 h, and 24 h. [plan: full]
