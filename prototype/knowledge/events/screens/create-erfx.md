## What each control on create-erfx does

- **Ask Clara** (button): Clicking it opens Clara's assistance panel. Affects: nothing until Clara generates or applies content to the event.. Leads to: Clara summary panel. Rule R-FCR-042-4. Check: Click Ask Clara and verify that the Clara panel opens.
- **Add Attachments** (button): Clicking it opens the attachment interface so the buyer can add files. Affects: the event's attachment data.. Leads to: attachment drawer or dialog. Check: Click Add Attachments and verify that an interface for selecting files appears.
- **Add T&Cs** (button): Clicking it opens the terms-and-conditions editor. Affects: the event's terms_and_conditions data.. Leads to: terms-and-conditions drawer or dialog. Rule R-FCR-008-3. Check: Click Add T&Cs and verify that the terms editor opens.
- **Items 0** (button): Clicking it opens the manage line items section. Affects: the event's line_items record, including added or deleted items.. Leads to: manage line items drawer or dialog. Rule R-FCR-014-3. Check: Click Items 0 and verify that the line-item manager opens and shows zero items.
- **Save as draft** (button, disabled): The disabled button cannot save the event as a draft. Affects: nothing because no draft is saved.. Leads to: stays here. Rule R-FCR-016-2. Check: Verify that Save as draft is disabled while no template and line items are present.
- **Publish** (button): Clicking it validates the event and, if validation succeeds, publishes it. Affects: the event and its stages, changing them from draft toward the published or active flow.. Leads to: validation feedback or the published event/project. Rule R-FCR-019-2. Check: Click Publish with the current incomplete form and verify that publishing is blocked with the applicable validation message.
- **Rank on lot** (button): Clicking it selects or opens the lot-level ranking configuration. Affects: the auction's ranking and lot-closing strategy.. Leads to: auction strategy configuration or stays here with the lot ranking option selected. Rule R-FCR-009-1. Check: Click Rank on lot and verify that the lot-based ranking setting is selected or its configuration opens.
- **Select Templates** (button): Clicking it opens template selection. Affects: the event's selected template and the configuration data derived from that template.. Leads to: template-selection drawer. Rule R-FCR-026-4. Check: Click Select Templates and verify that available templates are displayed.
- **Participants** (button): Clicking it opens participant management. Affects: the event's audience_company_uuids and participant records.. Leads to: participants drawer. Rule R-FCR-017-1. Check: Click Participants and verify that vendors or participants can be added or managed.
- **Savings** (button, disabled): The disabled button cannot open or edit savings settings. Affects: nothing because savings cannot currently be changed.. Leads to: stays here. Rule R-FCR-024-2. Check: Verify that Savings is disabled until a template and at least one product are present.
- **Terms & Conditions** (button, disabled): The disabled button cannot open or edit terms and conditions settings. Affects: nothing because the control is unavailable.. Leads to: stays here. Rule R-FCR-024-2. Check: Verify that Terms & Conditions is disabled while the required template and product prerequisites are missing.
- **Now - 02:33 am, 21st Oct ( 30 Mins )** (button, disabled): The disabled button cannot open or change the event schedule. Affects: nothing because the schedule cannot currently be edited from this control.. Leads to: stays here. Rule R-FCR-024-2. Check: Verify that the schedule control is disabled before a template and product are added.
- **Auction** (tab): Clicking the selected Auction tab displays the auction configuration stage. Affects: nothing beyond the currently displayed stage configuration.. Leads to: Auction tab content. Rule R-FCR-009-1. Check: Click Auction and verify that it remains selected and its configuration is visible.
- **Events** (link): Clicking it navigates away from event creation to the events area. Affects: nothing in the event data.. Leads to: Events screen. Check: Click Events and verify that the events list or events landing screen opens.
- **Enter the title of the project** (field): Entering text sets the project title. Affects: the event group's title and the title used in publish validation and confirmation.. Leads to: stays here. Rule R-FCR-013-1. Check: Enter a title and verify that the field retains it and the title is used when publishing.
- **Search vendors you want to add** (select, disabled): The disabled vendor search cannot be used to search for or add vendors. Affects: nothing because audience participants cannot currently be changed through this field.. Leads to: stays here. Rule R-FCR-016-2. Check: Verify that the vendor search is disabled until the required template and line-item prerequisites are met.
